Common questions.

Which file formats do you accept?

Vector files work best: AI, PDF, DXF or CDR with artwork set up for cutting. Only have a sketch? Our design desk will redraw it into a clean cutting file.

Is there a minimum order value?

No. One replacement letter or a single prototype gets the same care as a production batch — per-piece pricing simply improves with quantity.

Can you cut metal?

Our CO2 laser handles non-metals, and the router works on aluminium composite panels. Thin sheet-metal jobs we outsource to trusted partners and finish in-house.

How do edges finish on cut acrylic?

Laser edges come out flame-polished and glossy. Routered edges are finished smooth and can be buffed where the part is visible up close.
