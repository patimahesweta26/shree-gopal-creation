Common questions.

How should I prepare my file?

CMYK PDF or high-res JPG/TIFF at final size with 3–5 mm bleed. Not sure? Send whatever you have — we check every file before it hits the machine.

What's the smallest quantity?

One copy. That is the point of digital printing — no plates, no minimums. Rates per piece improve as quantities grow.

Can you print my design on a board too?

Yes. Print, lamination and mounting on foam, Sunboard or ACP happen under the same roof, so nothing shifts colour between vendors.

Do you offer design help?

Our desk can lay out text, fix logos and resize artwork for different formats at reasonable rates before printing.

Send the file. Hold the print tomorrow.

Most standard jobs are same-day or next-day. Tell us the deadline and we will confirm honestly.
