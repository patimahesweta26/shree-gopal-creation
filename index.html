What customers say

Questions we hear every week.

How much does an LED sign board cost?

It depends on size, material and lighting. As a rough guide, illuminated shop boards start around ₹800 per sq ft and rise with acrylic thickness, aluminium channels and detailing. Send your board size or artwork and we will quote the same day.

How long does a sign take from approval to installation?

Most boards are fabricated and installed within 3 to 7 working days after you approve the design proof. If you have a hard launch date, tell us upfront and we will plan backwards from it.

Do you help with signage permissions?

We advise on the common requirements for shop signage in Bhubaneswar and Khurda, and every installation is structurally fixed and wired safely by our own crew.

Which materials do you work with?

Acrylic sheets in 3 mm and 5 mm, ACP, GI and aluminium profiles, branded LED modules, vinyl films and flex from 280 to 450 GSM — all cut and printed on our own machines.

Do you install outside Bhubaneswar?

Yes, we install across Odisha. Transport and on-site travel beyond Bhubaneswar is billed at actuals and confirmed before the job begins, so there are no surprises on the final bill.
