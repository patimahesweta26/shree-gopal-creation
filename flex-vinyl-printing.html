Common questions.

How long will my outdoor banner last?

With outdoor inks on decent star-flex, expect one to two monsoon seasons of good colour. Permanent hoardings do better on 450 GSM with a proper frame — we recommend what fits the site, not what costs more.

My last banner tore at the eyelets. Why?

Usually over-stretched tying and wide eyelet spacing. We hem the edges, space eyelets to the actual hanging points and add corner patches on big sizes so wind does not do the tearing for you.

Can you print and paste on the same visit?

Yes. Printing happens in Khurda and our pasting crew covers Bhubaneswar daily. Glass fronts, pillars and vehicle branding are routine work.

Do you take urgent festival orders?

Festival weeks run hot here. Message us early with quantities and we reserve machine time; same-day is often possible for standard sizes.
