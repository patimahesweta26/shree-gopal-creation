Common questions.

What decides the price of an LED board?

Board area, acrylic thickness, whether letters are individually cut or mounted on a facing panel, and the lighting behind them. Send us your board size and we will quote the same day.

Glow sign or 3D acrylic letters — which should I pick?

Glow signs are economical and read well from a distance. Cut acrylic letters look premium up close and suit showrooms, clinics and cafés. We will show you samples of both before you decide.

Will it raise my electricity bill?

Modern LED modules draw very little power — a typical shop-front board adds less load than two ceiling fans, usually run on a timer for evening hours only.

Do you repair boards you did not make?

Yes. Rewiring, module replacement and face repairs are routine workshop jobs regardless of who built the original board.
