Privacy & image credits.

The short version: this site collects nothing behind your back, and the enquiry form only opens WhatsApp with what you typed.

What we collect

Nothing on our servers. This website has no database, no analytics and no cookies. When you use the enquiry form, your browser opens WhatsApp with the name, phone number, service and message you entered, and you choose whether to send it.

What happens after you message us

Your WhatsApp message and any files you share are handled by Shree Gopal Creations purely to quote and deliver your job. We do not sell or share your details with anyone else.

Hosting

This site is served as static files by GitHub Pages. Standard server logs are maintained by GitHub under their own privacy policy; we have no access to personal data in them.

Contact about privacy

Questions or requests: email info@shreegopalcreations.com or call 073151 27341.
